Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything is free — no signup, no watermark, no paid tier. Design, style and export as many codes as you like; the whole tool runs in your browser and is open source under the MIT license.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. Every QR you design here points directly at your link and works forever — there's no redirect, nothing routes through us, and nothing to keep alive.

Will my logo stop it from scanning?

No — QR codes carry built-in error correction, so a centred logo and styling still scan reliably. We keep the quiet margin and contrast safe while you design.

Can I redesign a QR I already have?

Yes. Switch to the “Existing QR” tab and upload a photo or screenshot — we read the code and load it into the editor so you can restyle the ugly version.

Which format should I download?

Use SVG for anything printed large (posters, signage, packaging) since it stays sharp at any size. Use PNG or JPG for screens, social posts and quick prints.
